Reference Sheet
👁️ Eye Measurements
Near point = 25 cm
Far point = ∞ (normal)
Diameter of eye ≈ 2.3 cm
Rods: ~120 million, dim light, B&W
Cones: ~6 million, colour, bright light
Blind spot: optic disc (no receptors)
👓 Defect Correction Summary
Myopia: Concave lens, P = −1/d (m)
Hypermetropia: Convex lens, P > 0
Presbyopia: Bifocal lens
Astigmatism: Cylindrical lens
Power unit: Dioptre (D) = m⁻¹
🌈 Dispersion & Scattering
I ∝ 1/λ⁴ (Rayleigh scattering)
VIBGYOR (Violet bends most)
Rainbow: Red 42°, Violet 40°
Blue sky: short λ scattered → blue reaches us
Red sunset: long path, blue scattered away
Tyndall: colloids scatter visible light
🌅 Atmospheric Refraction Facts
Advance sunrise: ~2 min early
Delayed sunset: ~2 min late
Extra daylight: ~4 min/day
Stars twinkle (point source)
Planets don't twinkle (disc source)
Sun appears oval at horizon
