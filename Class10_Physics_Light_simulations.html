🪞 Law of Reflection
Class 10 Physics · Ch 10
🏠
Controls
Measurements
∠i (Incidence) = 40°
∠r (Reflection) = 40°
∠i = ∠r ✓
Law of Reflection:
∠i = ∠r
Incident ray, reflected ray & normal are coplanar
Key Points
• Angles are measured from the Normal (perpendicular to surface)

• The incident ray, normal, and reflected ray all lie in the same plane

• Regular reflection → parallel rays remain parallel (smooth surface)

• Diffuse reflection → scattered rays (rough surface)

💧 Snell's Law — Refraction
Class 10 Physics · Ch 10
🏠
Incident Angle
Medium (below boundary)
Measurements
—
Snell's Law:
n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
n = c / v
Air (n₁ = 1.0) → denser medium

• Light bends toward normal when entering denser medium
• Higher refractive index = more bending
• Critical angle → Total Internal Reflection
